## Project Idea

**Pet Buddy** is a pet-care platform designed to make pet ownership easier, more organized, and accessible.

The platform aims to help pet owners manage their pets' daily needs and discover essential pet-care services in one place.

From finding nearby veterinary clinics and pet shops to organizing feeding schedules, vaccination reminders, and veterinary appointments, Pet Buddy aims to make pet care simpler and more convenient.

Our vision is to create a connected, pet-friendly community where every pet receives the care, attention, and love it deserves.

## Work Plan

Our project workflow includes the following phases:

1. **Research & Brainstorming:** Identifying the problem and exploring possible solutions.
2. **Project Planning:** Defining the project concept, objectives, and target audience.
3. **Feature Definition:** Identifying the essential features and functionalities.
4. **Visual Design:** Creating the visual identity and required design assets.
5. **Development:** Building and implementing the project.
6. **Testing & Improvement:** Reviewing functionality, identifying issues, and making improvements.
7. **Final Presentation:** Preparing and presenting the completed project.
